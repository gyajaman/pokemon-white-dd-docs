Pokemon White DD

A Pokemon White mod: every battle is a double, with a level cap by badge count and a new doubles AI. Encounter tables and trainer battles for Nuzlocke runs.

  • Encounters: wild tables per area, and gifts.
  • Trainers: every battle, with moves.
  • Move changes and Pokemon changes.

Rules of the mod

  • All battles are doubles; trainers cannot be avoided; the party never drops below 2.
  • Level cap by badges: 14, 20, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 50.
  • Trainers never use items from their bag.
  • Repel and Infinite Candy are key items. The Day Care is closed.
