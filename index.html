Pokemon White DD

A Pokemon White mod: every battle is a double, with a level cap by badge count and a new doubles AI. Encounter tables, trainer battles and item locations for planning a run.

Covered so far: Nuvema Town to the Nimbasa gym (Gym 4). Everything up to the Nacrene gym (Gym 2) is settled. Gym 2 to Gym 4 is a first draft from 2026-11-16 that has not been play-tested.

  • Encounters: wild tables per area (grass, dark grass, rustling grass), and gifts.
  • Trainers: every battle with moves, abilities and items, one page per stretch.
  • Items: held items on the ground, key items, Lucky Eggs.
  • Move changes and Pokemon changes.

Rules of the mod

  • All battles are doubles; trainers cannot be avoided; the party never drops below 2.
  • Level cap by badges (0 to 8): 14, 20, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 50.
  • Trainers never use items from their bag.
  • Repel and Infinite Candy are key items. The Day Care is closed.
  • Battle style is Set; Shift is disabled.
