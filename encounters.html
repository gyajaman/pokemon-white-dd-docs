Wild encounters

Percentages are the chance of each Pokemon once an encounter triggers. Levels hardly matter: the Infinite Candy raises any Pokemon to the badge cap for free. Dark grass gives double wild battles and has its own table. From Route 3 on, nothing in the grass is rarer than 10%.

First draft: everything on this page from Gym 2 onward was added in one pass on 2026-11-16 and has not been play-tested. Expect changes.
